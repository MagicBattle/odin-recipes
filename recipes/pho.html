Ingredients

First gather beef bones
Next gather any choice of meat
Then gather some vegetables
In addition gather some seasonings
Get some fish sauce as well
Finally get some rock sugar

Cooking Progress

First you need to clean the bones/meats
Second you need to add all the ingredients and simmer the brother
Char the vegetables while you wait for the broth to simmer
Add the finishing touches to the broth with fish sauce and rock sugar
Enjoy your delicous bowl of pho!
